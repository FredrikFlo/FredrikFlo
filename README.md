**TTT4270-Elsys-Prosjekt - README**  
This project was one that took the course of a whole semester. We worked in a group of 6 and wanted to make a ticket system for an AutoFerry project by NTNU. This was to ensure safety of boarding as well as passenger counting along with many other efforts of digitilisation. It was an ambitious project where we dipped our toes into the unkown waters of BLE (Bluetooth Low-Energy). My job with the help of a peer was to design the app that was going to drive this operation. This involved learning an entirely new piece of software called Android Studio as well as applying my C++ knowledge to a completely new language called Kotlin. Together me and my colleague designed an app that allowed the user to create a unique ticket in the form of a QR-code and further scan said QR-code to then be connected via BLE to the ferry. This project required app design, QR-code generation, and BLE advertising. We faced a lot of obstacles during the way, but we managed to push through and get a working app in time for the project deadline.
